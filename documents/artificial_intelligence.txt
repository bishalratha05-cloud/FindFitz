Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence is a field of computer science focused on building systems
that perform tasks commonly associated with human intelligence. These tasks include
learning, reasoning, problem solving, language understanding and perception.

Machine learning is a major area of artificial intelligence. A machine learning
system learns patterns from examples instead of relying only on rules written by
a programmer. Deep learning uses neural networks with many layers to learn complex
patterns from large datasets.

AI systems are used in healthcare, education, transportation, agriculture and
customer support. Responsible AI development considers fairness, privacy,
security, transparency and the effect of automation on society.
