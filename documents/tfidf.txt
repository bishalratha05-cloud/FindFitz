TF-IDF and Cosine Similarity

Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency, known as TF-IDF, is a numerical statistic that reflects how important a word is to a document in a collection. Term frequency measures how often a term appears in a document. Inverse document frequency reduces the weight of terms that occur in many documents.

Cosine similarity measures the cosine of the angle between two vectors. In information retrieval, it can compare a query vector with document vectors. A higher cosine similarity score indicates a closer match between the query and document representations.
